Why Online Safety Matters

Classified networks like Locanto offer great utilities for connecting people, but navigating them requires high digital awareness. While many profiles are genuine, bad actors and automated bots often set up traps. This independent community guideline is built to train you on how to protect your identity, privacy, and finances.

1. Smart Profile Verification

  • Analyze Pictures: Run a quick reverse-image search if a user's profile pictures look overly processed, professional, or artificial.
  • Keep Info Private: Never share sensitive assets like your home address, workplace, or personal email address early in a chat.
  • Use Built-in Chats: Avoid migrating immediately to external chat clients or anonymous social media handles.

2. Financial Red Flags

  • Zero Upfront Money: Cut communication immediately if an individual requests advance cash for travel, hotel deposits, or medical urgency.
  • Link Verification: Do not click on external third-party transactional interfaces or phishing setups sent through messages.
  • No Gift Cards/Crypto: Requests for unconventional payments like crypto or gift cards are immediate indicators of a scam setup.

3. Safe Offline Meetups

  • Public Spaces Only: Always plan your first casual meeting during daylight hours in crowded public domains like cafes or malls.
  • Inform Someone: Ensure a close friend or family member knows exactly where you are heading and who you are meeting.
  • Independent Transit: Arrange your own commute to and from the venue. Never rely on the other person for a lift on a first date.
